The Equestrian Aesthetic

Styled by Tabitha Simmons

With Tabitha Simmons, the house is composing a new equestrian aesthetic — for day, competition and evening.

The World of Kiwi & Colibri

Kiwi & Colibri was born from my connection with the horses, from my desire to step into the competition ring as one with them. I wanted to reflect their beauty without taking their light — to stand beside them as a mirror.

When someone wears Kiwi & Colibri, something shifts. In how they carry themselves, how they approach their horse, how they move through the world. They become the person who wears Kiwi & Colibri.

This is the heritage of the house: born from the bond between horse and rider, refined through couture, and created for those who recognize themselves in it.

— Sofia Ek, Founder & Creative Director

The Name

A kiwi looks ordinary until you cut it open — then it is a brilliant green you never forget. A colibri hovers perfectly still while its wings beat endlessly. The horse looks simple until it moves; the rider looks still but makes countless adjustments. What looks ordinary at first is extraordinary in motion.

Atelier

The atelier floor — Cieffe, Milan

The Equestrian Suit

In Milan, at Cieffe, tailoring is discipline. Every line is tested, every seam refined, until it carries the presence I demand.

For three years my seamstress, Emily, sat in their ateliers — ensuring each detail of the equestrian suit was shaped without compromise.

Our garments must be as beautiful on the inside as on the outside. Like a partnership with a horse, they must be truthful and honest.

Created to be kept, handed down, never discarded.

Craftsmanship

The Story of Colibri Crêpe

Our cloth is woven at Reggiani in Biella — a family mill founded in 1973, pioneers in combining heritage and modernity.

It was a long journey. In the first year, only an idea: one fabric to carry an entire equestrian suit.

In the second year, we believed we had it. The drape was right, the weight assured. But in the saddle, the fabric did not yet understand the conversation between horse and rider. So we began again.

By the third year we reached our goal —

A woven crêpe with life in its surface. Matte, textured, enduring. It moves as the rider moves, yet carries its presence wherever it is worn.

Exclusive to the house.
